Bheramara — attractions & why they're special

The closest sights to the resort — most reachable in 15–30 minutes.

🌉Recommended

Hardinge Bridge

A 100+ year-old steel railway bridge built during the British era, standing parallel to the modern Lalon Shah Road Bridge. Together they cross the mighty Padma River — a historical engineering marvel.

🏛️ Heritage🌉 Landmark
View Details →
🌊Recommended

Lalon Shah Bridge

The modern road bridge running parallel to Hardinge Bridge over the Padma. Sunset views, river breezes, and one of the best photography spots in the district.

📸 Photography🌅 Sunset

Ganges-Kobodak (G-K) Project & Water Pump Station

One of Bangladesh's largest historic irrigation projects — massive water intake channels and pumping infrastructure surrounded by lush greenery along the Padma River banks.

🌊 Nature🏛️ Heritage

Moni Park & Green Valley Park

Family-friendly amusement parks in the Bheramara region, featuring rides, landscaped gardens, lakes and picnic spots — a relaxed midday stop between sightseeing.

📖Heritage

Shilaidaha Rabindra Kuthibari

The country residence of Nobel laureate poet Rabindranath Tagore, in Kumarkhali. A three-story brick mansion housing preserved artifacts, original furniture, and personal belongings where Tagore composed many of his famous poems and Gitanjali verses.

🏛️ Heritage📖 Literature
🎶Cultural

Lalon Akhara / Shrine of Fakir Lalon Shah

In Chhewuriya — the resting place and spiritual sanctuary of the legendary Baul saint Fakir Lalon Shah, and the heart of Baul philosophy, music and mystical traditions in Bengal.

🎶 Baul Culture🙏 Spiritual
🏛️Heritage

Mir Mosharraf Hossain Memorial

In Lahinipara — the ancestral home and memorial museum dedicated to Mir Mosharraf Hossain, author of the classic Bengali novel Bishad Sindhu.

🏛️ Heritage📖 Literature
